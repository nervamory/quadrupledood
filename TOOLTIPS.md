# Keywords

A word in a card line that matches one of these is bold, and a reminder box is shown under the tooltip. `Also matches` lists other spellings that use the same box.

## orthogonal
top, bottom, left, right directions

## touching
all 8 adjacent squares, including diagonals

## blood
a cell left when heart, brain, or bat is captured

## retrigger
that card's play ability happens again

Also matches: retriggers

# Cards

One line per card. Card names are the ids used in game.
